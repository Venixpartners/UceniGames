2. What the service is

Uceni gives you timed practice questions across subjects such as Mathematics, English Language, General Science, Nigerian History and Civic Education, Geography, Health and Wellbeing, Technology and General Knowledge. After each question you see the correct answer and a short explanation.

Uceni is a learning service. Your subscription pays only for access to the learning content and practice platform.

Your results depend only on your own answers.
