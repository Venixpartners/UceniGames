2. What the service is

Uceni gives you timed practice questions across subjects such as Mathematics, English Language, General Science, Nigerian History and Civic Education, Geography, Health and Wellbeing, Technology and General Knowledge. After each question you see the correct answer and a short explanation.

Each session has 10 questions at the level you choose. Beginner allows 30 seconds per question, Intermediate 20 seconds and Advanced 15 seconds. Harder levels earn more Knowledge Points for each correct answer.

Uceni is a learning service. During the free preview, gameplay is available without payment.

3. Knowledge Points

Correct answers, quick answers, completed sessions and daily practice earn Knowledge Points. Knowledge Points measure your learning progress and nothing else. They have no monetary value and cannot be bought, sold, redeemed, exchanged or transferred for cash, airtime, data, goods or material prizes. Uceni offers no monetary or material prizes. Your results depend on your own answers.

Optional gamified features include daily learning missions, virtual achievement badges, subject mastery stages, personal-best records, avatars, weekly educational leaderboards and seven-day friend challenges. Badges are for recognition only, cannot be exchanged or transferred for value and do not increase billing. When you share a friend challenge, anyone holding its link can view the display name and score associated with that completed round.
