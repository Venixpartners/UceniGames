Privacy Notice

Last updated 18 November 2026

This notice explains what personal data Uceni Games collects, why, and the choices you have. Venix Partners Limited is the data controller.

Current free preview: We ask you to enter a mobile number to use the free game, but we do not verify it, use it to identify an existing account, or save the entered number to our learner database. Your game progress belongs to an independently generated guest session in this browser. There are currently no airtime or subscription charges. You cannot yet recover free-preview progress using your mobile number.

We do not collect your bank details, card numbers, BVN or NIN.

2. Who we share it with

During the free preview, no subscriber number is sent to a billing aggregator for charging. Our hosting and database providers process guest accounts and gameplay data on our behalf. We may disclose information to competent authorities where legally required. We do not sell personal data.

3. Where your data is stored

Our database and hosting providers may store data outside Nigeria, including in the United Kingdom and the European Union. Where this happens we rely on the safeguards the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 requires.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while you have an account and for up to 12 months after you last use Uceni. We keep billing, consent and complaint records for up to 6 years, because the law and our licence require it.

5. Keeping it safe

All traffic uses encrypted connections. Access to learner data is limited to authorised staff. Guest-session tokens are stored as hashes in our database. SMS sign-in codes are not sent during the free preview. The session token is saved in this browser, so only use devices you trust.

6. Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of personal data, and choose to hide yourself from the learning board. During the free preview, your entered mobile number does not identify your guest account; contact hello@venixpartners.com while signed in so our team can explain how to handle your request without exposing another person's account. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

7. Messages we send

No subscription confirmation, renewal or charge messages are sent during the free preview. If paid services launch, they will require separate, verified consent and updated disclosures.

8. Children

Subscribers must be 18 or older. Where a parent or guardian allows a younger learner to use their subscription, the parent or guardian is responsible for that use.

9. Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we will tell you on the site before the change takes effect.
