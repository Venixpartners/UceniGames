Privacy Notice

Last updated 26 September 2026

This notice explains what personal data Uceni Games collects, why, and the choices you have. We process your data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the Commission's consumer rules. Venix Partners Limited is the data controller.

We do not collect your bank details, card numbers, BVN or NIN.

2. Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with your mobile network and our licensed billing aggregator to process subscriptions and charges, our hosting and database providers who store data on our behalf under contract, and the Nigerian Communications Commission or other authorities when the law requires. We never sell your data or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

3. Where your data is stored

Our database and hosting providers may store data outside Nigeria, including in the United Kingdom and the European Union. Where this happens we rely on the safeguards the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 requires.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while you have an account and for up to 12 months after you last use Uceni. We keep billing, consent and complaint records for up to 6 years, because the law and our licence require it.

5. Keeping it safe

All traffic uses encrypted connections. Access to learner data is limited to staff who need it. Sign in codes and session keys are stored in scrambled form so they cannot be read even from our own database.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy, or object to how we use it. You can also withdraw consent, for example by hiding yourself from the learning board in Account. Email hello@venixpartners.com with your mobile number and we will respond within 30 days. If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

7. Messages we send

We send service messages about your subscription only: confirmations, receipts, renewal reminders and cancellations. We do not send promotional messages to numbers on the Do Not Disturb register.

8. Children

Subscribers must be 18 or older. Where a parent or guardian allows a younger learner to use their subscription, the parent or guardian is responsible for that use.

9. Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we will tell you on the site before the change takes effect.
