Service Policies

Last updated 26 September 2026

These policies sit alongside our Terms of Service and explain how we handle billing, refunds, content, fair use and complaints.

1. Subscription and Billing Policy

  • Every subscription needs your clear request and a second confirmation before any charge.
  • Every consent request states the service name, price, access period, renewal frequency and how to cancel.
  • We charge no more than once for each access period.
  • If your balance is too low, no partial charge is taken.
  • Weekly and monthly subscribers get a reminder before renewal.
  • Any free trial or promotion will never turn into a paid plan without fresh consent.
  • All prices and promotions are approved by the Nigerian Communications Commission before use.

2. Refund Policy

We refund in full any charge that was taken without valid consent, any duplicate charge for the same period, and any charge taken after you cancelled. To ask for a refund, send your mobile number and the date of the charge through any complaint channel below. We confirm eligibility within 72 hours and pass approved refunds to your network or aggregator for credit to your line. Charges for periods you subscribed to and could use are not refundable once the period has started, but you can cancel at any time to stop future charges.

3. Acceptable Use Policy

To keep Uceni fair for every learner, you must not:

  • share your account or sign in code with anyone else;
  • use bots, scripts or other automated tools to answer questions;
  • copy, scrape or republish our questions and explanations;
  • try to break, overload or bypass the security of the service;
  • use a display name that is offensive, misleading or impersonates someone else.

We may reset Knowledge Points gained unfairly, change offensive display names, or suspend accounts that break these rules. We will tell you why.

4. Content Standards Policy

  • Questions follow the Nigerian school curriculum and reliable general knowledge sources.
  • Content is factual, neutral and respectful of every ethnic, religious and cultural group.
  • We do not publish political campaigning, hate speech, adult content, violence or anything that promotes gambling.
  • Every question is reviewed and approved before learners see it.
  • Questions reported by learners are reviewed promptly and corrected or withdrawn if wrong.

5. No Prizes Policy

Uceni Games is a learning service. We do not offer, promise or award cash, airtime, data, goods, vouchers or any other prize or reward based on Knowledge Points, ranks, difficulty levels, streaks or position on the learning board. The learning board exists for motivation only. Our adverts and messages never suggest otherwise.

7. Cookies and Device Storage

Uceni does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We store one small sign in key on your device so you stay signed in. You can remove it at any time by signing out or clearing your browser data.

8. Accessibility

We designed Uceni to be easy to read on small screens, using a typeface made for legibility, strong contrast and support for screen readers and keyboards. If something is hard to use, tell us through any complaint channel and we will work to fix it.
